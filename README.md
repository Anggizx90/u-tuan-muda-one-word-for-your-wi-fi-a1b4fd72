Entry for make.fun's **One Word for Your Wi-Fi** bounty ($2 winner-take-all).
The program writes one word describing the entrant's Wi-Fi to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The word

**Diva**

## Why "Diva" — mapped to the judge's five criteria, in order

1. **On-subject, Wi-Fi as lived:** a diva only performs on her own terms and
   storms off mid-show — exactly how home Wi-Fi behaves: full bars but nothing
   loads, dies mid-call, works only in one room.
2. **Funny at once:** picturing the router as a temperamental starlet who
   refuses to perform unless conditions are perfect lands instantly, with no
   explanation needed.
3. **True to home Wi-Fi:** everyone has lived it — the connection that needs a
   daily restart, throws a tantrum when too many devices show up, and only
   works when you stand in the one blessed spot near the router.
4. **Fresh angle:** it skips the words everyone reaches for (slow, weak,
   unreliable, flaky) and personifies the router as a character with demands
   instead of describing a signal.
5. **Works as a single word:** four letters, spelled the way everyone knows it,
   readable at a glance — and the shortest strong candidate, which also wins
   exact ties on size.

## Shortlist — candidates considered and rejected

| Word | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Teenager | Runner-up: relatable and funny (moody, ignores you, slams the door), but a familiar tech metaphor and twice the bytes of Diva. |
| Clingy | True and specific (clings to a dying signal instead of switching), but the laugh is a smile, not a laugh. |
| Goldilocks | Fresh angle (only works when everything is just right, in one exact spot), but 10 letters and the joke needs the fairy tale. |
| Hermit | Good image (never leaves the router's room), but too quiet — more wry than funny. |
| Moody | True, but the first word everyone reaches for; fails freshness. |
| Flaky | Accurate, but generic and overused for anything unreliable. |
| Needy | True (needs a daily restart, constant attention), but reads as a complaint, not a laugh. |
| Picky | Short and plausible, but the joke is thin — picky about what, exactly? |
| Introvert | Fresh (can't handle a house full of guests), but the laugh lands slower than Diva's. |
| Dramatic | On the right track, but vague — dramatic how? Needs the story to land. |
| Sleepy | Cute, but weak: falling asleep mid-stream is only one failure mode. |
| Forgetful | True-ish (drops saved networks), but forgetfulness is a device trait, not the Wi-Fi's. |
| Jealous | Amusing (drops when another device connects), but the metaphor strains — Wi-Fi isn't jealous, it's overwhelmed. |
| Narcoleptic | Funny image (falls asleep mid-call), but clinical and 11 letters; obscurity costs it. |
| Faint | Short, but reads as a signal-strength report, not a joke. |
| Vanishing | Rejected: too close to the already-submitted Ghosting / peekaboo disappearing concept. |
| Elusive | Rejected: same disappearing concept as Ghosting, and less funny. |
| Temperamental | Accurate, but 13 letters and the generic version of what Diva says better. |

Words already submitted by others and avoided: Gaslighting, peekaboo, Liar,
Ghosting, Wi-Fickle, Bathroom, Bold Meridian, Fresh Delta — plus near-duplicates
of their concepts (deception, disappearing, fickleness).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes "Diva" (exactly, no trailing newline) to /output/entry.txt
```

Node 22, no network, no dependencies, deterministic: every run writes the
same 4 bytes.
